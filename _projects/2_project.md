## Adding affective control without losing realism

StableFace is a fine-tuned diffusion model for generating photorealistic faces with clearer control over emotional expression. The work started from a practical limitation in general-purpose image models: visual quality can be high while nuanced affect remains inconsistent.

I optimized the model with a perceptual-loss objective across a large facial-emotion corpus, using eight emotion categories. The broader research program covered **500,000 images** and improved task accuracy by **25%**.

The project explores the trade-off between two objectives that often compete in generative vision:

- preserving the base model's visual fidelity;
- making the requested expression legible and consistent.
